# Third-party data and tools

GraceLoop contains adapters for ToolUniverse, GraceGraph, and a registered
PhytoPerturb proof. Those adapters do not grant a license to the external tools or to
records returned by NCBI, PubMed, Europe PMC, Plant Reactome, OMA, InterPro, or any
other provider.

The repository's public demonstration is synthetic and network-free. It intentionally
does not bundle the earlier live CKX database projections. Anyone publishing a live
receipt or capsule must review the exact projected fields, retain source provenance,
comply with current provider terms, and add path-scoped attribution outside the
tamper-evident capsule.

Hashes and accession labels establish byte identity or documentary linkage only. They
do not establish biological truth, rights, coordinate equivalence, or permission to
perform an experiment.
